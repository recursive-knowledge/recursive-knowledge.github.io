“How does self-improving knowledge work?” — design candidates

All three contenders use the paper’s vocabulary: 5 beats (Seed → Attempt → Task-level forum → Cross-task forum → Distillation), the two forum stages shown as real threads and marked as the method’s core, and the shared knowledge base as the thing that improves. They differ mainly in how the base’s improvement is dramatized.

Interactive — pause/restart, click steps. Numbers illustrative.

Design 1 · Loop + central vault open ↗
Loop ring with the base living at the center; a pulse travels the loop, the base fills with insight “spines,” the % counts up with a “+N” flash, and a token flies in at Distillation. Improvement = the center filling + number jumping.
Design 2 · Loop-as-gauge open ↗
No traveling pulse — instead the whole loop ring charges with accent as the base improves (nodes light in sequence). Improvement = the ring visibly filling toward complete, generation by generation.
Design 3 · Pipeline + growing base open ↗
Drops the ring: the protocol reads left-to-right as a pipeline, and a hero base panel on the right fills dramatically — insight cards stack up, a meter rises, the % climbs. Most literal “watch it grow.”
